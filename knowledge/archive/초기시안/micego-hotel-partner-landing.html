01_WHY PARTNER

Group business, without the cold outreach

Three things MICE sales teams tell us.

Enquiries arrive half-formed

Group leads often reach you with no confirmed dates, no room split and no banquet brief. Your team quotes into uncertainty, or spends a week chasing the basics.

The Korean market is hard to reach

Korean agencies and corporates plan overseas incentive and conference programs year-round, but reaching them means cold outreach, roadshows and layers of intermediaries.

You quote, then hear nothing

Requests get shopped around informally, with no stated deadline and no word on where your proposal landed.

02_HOW IT WORKS

Three steps

No dashboard to learn, no subscription to manage.

01

Register your property

Tell us where you are, the group size you can host, and who to contact. Registration takes about a minute.

02

Receive matched requests

When an organizer's confirmed-date requirement matches your destination and capacity, we email you the standard brief — room split, banquet needs, dates, deadline.

03

Submit your quote

Respond on the request page with your rates and terms. MICEGO compiles all responses into one comparison for the organizer, and introduces you directly if you are selected.

No obligation

Every request is an invitation, not a commitment. Ignore the ones that don't fit; it doesn't affect the requests you receive next.

04_PARTNER TERMS

Partner terms

What we commit to, and what we ask.

No listing fee to join or to receive requests
You choose which requests to quote on
Confirmed dates only no speculative enquiries

Any destination outside Korea

We source for overseas programs only. Any country or city qualifies — there is no preferred-destination list.

Organizer identity withheld

Company name and budget stay confidential until the organizer selects a proposal. Everyone quotes on the same stated requirements.

No minimum property size

Boutique properties and large resorts are matched the same way — on destination, capacity and facilities.

Every request states its deadline

You always see the response window before you start pricing, and you can revise your quote until it closes.

What we ask

Quote on the requirements as written, and keep your rates valid through the date you state. That is what makes side-by-side comparison possible — and it is the reason organizers come back.

How MICEGO is paid

MICEGO charges a 10% commission on the room-block value of bookings confirmed through the platform. Nothing is charged for joining, for receiving requests, or for quoting — the commission applies only once a booking is confirmed through MICEGO.

15–25% Typical OTA group commission
8–12% Typical MICE DMC / agent commission
10% MICEGO — confirmed bookings only, 0 upfront
