Part 1 — General

Article 1

Purpose and parties

1.1 MICEGO is a reverse-auction sourcing service operated by (legal entity name — to be confirmed) (trading as MatchGo, “MICEGO”, “we”, “us”). It sends confirmed-date MICE requirements from Korean organizers to overseas hotels and resorts, collects their quotes and presents them side by side so the organizer can choose.

1.2 These Partner Terms are an agreement between MICEGO and the hotel or resort that registers with MICEGO (“you”, the “Property”). The person who registers or submits a quote confirms that they are authorised to accept these terms for the Property.

1.3 Organizers use MICEGO under separate Korean-language terms of use. Nothing in these terms makes the organizer a party to them.

Article 2

Definitions

  • “Organizer” — a travel agency, company or institution (and its contact person) that plans an event outside Korea and submits a request through MICEGO.
  • “Request” — an organizer's sourcing request, identified by a reference code beginning with MG.
  • “Brief” — the standard requirements document MICEGO prepares from a request and sends to matching Properties. It never contains the organizer's company name, budget or contact details.
  • “Invitation” — an email from MICEGO asking you to quote on a Brief, with a personal link to the request page.
  • “Quote” — the rates, availability, inclusions, validity, cancellation terms and other details you submit for a request.
  • “Comparison sheet” — the side-by-side view of quotes that MICEGO prepares for the organizer.
  • “Selection” — the organizer's confirmed choice of one quote.
  • “Business day” — any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in the Republic of Korea. Unless stated otherwise, times are Korea Standard Time (KST, UTC+9).
  • “Regional Partner” — a local company that operates MICEGO requests in a destination on MICEGO's behalf and under its supervision. Where a request is handled by a Regional Partner, the request page and our emails name it. A Regional Partner acts for MICEGO; it is not a party to these terms.
Article 3

Changes to these terms

3.1 We may update these terms. We will email registered Properties and post the new version at least 7 days before it takes effect, or at least 30 days before if the change is materially adverse to you.

3.2 If you do not agree with a change, you may end your partnership under Article 16 before it takes effect. Quotes already submitted remain governed by the terms in force when you submitted them.

Part 2 — Partnership

Article 4

Eligibility and registration

4.1 We approve hotels and resorts located outside the Republic of Korea that can host groups of 50 or more and have a verified contact email, ideally on the Property's own domain. A ballroom or banquet space is not required; some programs need guest rooms only.

4.2 We review each registration and reply by email within 5 business days. We may ask for information to confirm that the Property exists and that the contact person represents it, and we may decline a registration that does not meet these criteria.

4.3 You must keep your registration details — property name, location, group capacity, banquet facilities and contact details — accurate and tell us promptly when they change.

Article 5

Fees and commercial terms

5.1 There is no fee to register your Property or to receive requests.

5.2 MICEGO charges a commission on each booking that results from a selection made through MICEGO. The commission rate for your Property is set when we approve it and is shown to you, with a link to accept it, in our approval email (the “Agreed Rate”). The Agreed Rate takes effect only when you accept it by pressing the accept button on that page; MICEGO records the time of acceptance and the version of these terms. We do not send you Invitations until you have accepted an Agreed Rate. The Agreed Rate applies to every booking that results from your quote on an Invitation sent while that rate was in effect. legal review

5.3 The commission is the Agreed Rate applied to the net contract value of the booking. Net contract value is the total agreed contract amount for guest rooms and for banquet, meeting and food-and-beverage services, excluding taxes and service charges. If the booking is reduced or cancelled, the commission is calculated on the amount finally payable to you under your contract with the organizer. legal review

5.4 We may propose a different rate for your Property. A new rate takes effect only after you accept it through a new link we email to you, and it applies only to Invitations sent after you accept it. Invitations already sent, and the bookings that result from them, keep the rate that applied when the Invitation was sent.

5.5 After the event has ended, MICEGO, or the MICEGO Regional Partner responsible for the region on MICEGO's behalf, invoices you for the commission based on the final contract value and collects it on MICEGO's behalf. You pay the invoice within 30 days of the invoice date. legal review

5.6 Your Agreed Rate is confidential between you and MICEGO. It is not shown to organizers and does not affect how your quote is displayed or compared, and you must not disclose it to organizers or to other Properties.

5.7 Any booking contract, deposit and payment terms are agreed directly between you and the organizer. MICEGO is not a party to that contract and does not collect, hold or process payments between you and the organizer.

Part 3 — Requests and quotes

Article 6

Invitations

6.1 Each Invitation is an invitation to quote, not an obligation. You choose which requests to quote on.

6.2 We send an Invitation by email when a request matches your destination, capacity and facilities. Each request goes to a few matching Properties, all of whom receive the same Brief.

6.3 The link in an Invitation is personal to your Property and needs no login. Do not forward it. If a colleague should quote, ask us and we will send them their own link. Opening the link marks the Invitation as viewed.

6.4 Treat the Brief as confidential and use it only to prepare your quote.

Article 7

Deadlines

7.1 Every request states an exact quote deadline in KST, on the request page and in the Invitation. It is usually 3 business days after the Invitation at 18:00 KST, and 5 business days for groups of 200 or more. For events that are close, the deadline may be shorter.

7.2 We email a reminder 24 hours before the deadline. After the deadline the Invitation expires and the link no longer accepts quotes; if you quoted, it later shows the result.

7.3 If the organizer changes the requirements, we may re-invite you under a new deadline with a new link. Quotes made on earlier requirements are kept on record but are not used in the comparison.

Article 8

Quotes

8.1 Rates, availability and terms you submit must be accurate and made in good faith for the stated requirements.

8.2 Quote in the currency your Property invoices in. The organizer sees your rates in that currency. Where quotes on the same request are in different currencies, MICEGO adds a USD reference figure for the twin rate, using the market rate on the day it is entered and showing that date. This figure is for comparison only; contracting and payment stay in your currency.

8.3 State whether rates include taxes and service charge. If they do not, state the percentages so the organizer's estimate is calculated on the same basis as other quotes.

8.4 If you cannot offer every room, choose “Partially available” and describe what you can offer. Partial quotes are still shown to the organizer.

8.5 Your quote should remain valid until at least the date you state, which must be no earlier than 2 business days after the request's deadline.

8.6 You may revise your quote through the same link at any time before the deadline. Your latest submission replaces the earlier one.

8.7 A quote is not a booking contract. If you are selected, final terms are set in your contract with the organizer, and you should be prepared to contract on the rates and terms you quoted while the quote is valid, subject to availability you disclosed.

8.8 If your quotes are repeatedly inaccurate, we may pause your listing under Article 17.

8.9 Where you agree rates with MICEGO or a Regional Partner by phone, email or messenger instead of using the request page, we may enter that quote on your behalf. You then receive a single-use confirmation link valid for 72 hours. The quote is shown to the organizer only after you confirm it on that page; confirming has the same effect as submitting the quote yourself. If anything is wrong, flag it on the same page and the entered quote is withdrawn — you can still submit your own quote from your Invitation link before the deadline. If you neither confirm nor flag within 72 hours, the entered quote is not used. legal review

Article 9

Declining and unanswered invitations

9.1 You can decline a request in a few seconds using “Can't quote on this one?” on the request page. Declining never counts against you.

9.2 An Invitation left without a quote or a decline by the deadline counts as unanswered. After three consecutive unanswered Invitations we pause your listing and tell you by email. Reply and we will reinstate it.

Article 10

Questions about a request

10.1 Send questions about a request through the contact page, quoting the reference code. Do not try to contact the organizer directly.

10.2 MICEGO relays questions to the organizer and may share the question and answer, without identifying you, with every Property invited to the same request, so that everyone quotes on the same information.

Part 4 — Anonymity, selection and organizer data

Article 11

Anonymity before selection

11.1 The organizer's company name, budget and contact details are withheld from the Brief. Your Property name and contact details are likewise not shown to the organizer: the comparison sheet labels your quote as Proposal A, B or C, together with your star rating, area, distance to the beach, size and whether you are chain-affiliated.

11.2 You must not try to identify or contact the organizer before selection, or share the Brief or your quote with another Property.

Article 12

Selection and disclosure

12.1 After the organizer decides, MICEGO emails every Property that quoted. If you are not selected, you receive the result only; no organizer details are shared.

12.2 If you are selected, you receive the organizer's company name, contact name, email address and mobile number, together with any message the organizer wrote for you when selecting, and MICEGO introduces you to the organizer by email so you can continue directly. The request page also shows “Selected” with these details.

12.3 At the same time, your Property name and the contact details in your quote are disclosed to the organizer.

Article 13

Use of organizer data

13.1 You may use the organizer data you receive under Article 12 only to respond to the organizer, and to negotiate, contract and deliver the booking for that request.

13.2 You must not use that data for marketing, add it to mailing lists, sell it or disclose it to anyone except staff and service providers who need it for the booking and who are bound by confidentiality.

13.3 You must keep that data secure, handle it in line with the data-protection law that applies to you, and delete it when it is no longer needed for the booking or for records the law requires you to keep.

13.4 If you become aware of unauthorised access to, or disclosure of, organizer data you received through MICEGO, tell us without undue delay at mysteri1984@gmail.com.

Article 14

Non-circumvention

14.1 For 12 months after MICEGO introduces an organizer to you, you must not directly solicit that organizer, or accept a booking from it, for the event in the request — or for an event MICEGO first presented to you — in order to avoid the commission payable under Articles 5.2 to 5.5. legal review

14.2 This article does not restrict bookings with an organizer who was already your client before the introduction, or who approaches you independently of MICEGO for an unrelated event.

Article 15

Data protection

15.1 MICEGO is the controller of the organizer data it collects and of the personal data of your contact persons. Our Privacy Notice explains how we handle your contacts' data.

15.2 Once you receive organizer data under Article 12, you process it as an independent controller for the purposes in Article 13.1 and are responsible for complying with the data-protection laws that apply to you. MICEGO transfers this data to you with the organizer's consent obtained at selection. legal review

15.3 Each party will reasonably help the other respond to requests from individuals exercising their data-protection rights about the data exchanged under these terms.

15.4 You confirm that the contact persons whose details you give us have been told that MICEGO will process them as described in our Privacy Notice.

15.5 A Regional Partner processes your contact persons' data and the data in your quotes only on MICEGO's instructions, for the request it handles, under a written data-processing agreement with MICEGO. legal review

Part 5 — Unsubscribing, suspension and termination

Article 16

Unsubscribing and ending the partnership

16.1 Every Invitation contains an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing stops Invitation emails for your Property; selection results for quotes you already submitted are still sent. To receive Invitations again, contact us.

16.2 You may end your partnership at any time by emailing mysteri1984@gmail.com. We will stop sending Invitations and handle your contacts' data as described in our Privacy Notice.

16.3 Articles 5.2 to 5.7, 13, 14, 18 and 19 continue to apply after the partnership ends, for as long as they are relevant.

Article 17

Suspension

17.1 We may pause your listing if three consecutive Invitations go unanswered, or if your quotes are repeatedly inaccurate. We tell you by email and reinstate the listing when you reply and the reason has been addressed.

17.2 We may suspend or end your partnership, after telling you why, if you breach Articles 11, 13 or 14, if your registration details are found to be false, or if the Property no longer meets the eligibility criteria in Article 4.1.

Part 6 — Liability and law

Article 18

Responsibilities and liability

18.1 You are responsible for your quotes and for the booking contract, services and facilities you provide to the organizer. MICEGO does not guarantee that any request will result in a selection or a booking.

18.2 MICEGO provides the service with reasonable care. It is not responsible for the organizer's decisions, for the performance of any contract between you and the organizer, or for exchange-rate movements.

18.3 Neither party is liable to the other for indirect or consequential loss, or for loss of profit, arising from these terms. legal review

18.4 Each party's total liability to the other under these terms in any 12-month period is limited to the greater of (a) the commission paid or payable by you to MICEGO under Articles 5.2 to 5.5 in that period and (b) USD 1,000. legal review

18.5 Articles 18.3 and 18.4 do not limit liability for wilful misconduct or gross negligence, for breach of Articles 13 or 14, or any liability that cannot be limited by law.

18.6 Neither party is liable for failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, such as natural disasters, war, epidemics, government action or network failures.

Article 19

Governing law and disputes

19.1 These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Korea. legal review

19.2 The parties will first try to resolve any dispute through good-faith discussion by email. If a dispute is not resolved within 30 days, either party may bring it before the court having jurisdiction under the Civil Procedure Act of the Republic of Korea.
